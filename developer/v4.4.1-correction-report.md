## Scope

This correction pass addresses the two defects found during the V4.4 review without changing the threat model, combat normalization, weapon acquisition, awakening rules, Three Sword probabilities, or political bounty curve.

1. `requirementsFor()` now records only requirements explicitly defined by a discipline or technique. Unspecified requirements no longer inflate the passed/total denominator, success chance, or mastery.
2. Every successful discipline is retained in `combatProgression.disciplines`. The highest-power retained discipline remains the primary display, loadout, and direct combat contributor. Secondary disciplines can unlock techniques and appear in story/UI, but do not stack direct Combat Rating.
3. Technique evaluation now runs against every retained discipline.
4. A rare overlap between a partial Three Sword training result and the ordinary Two Sword route is deduplicated before persistence while retaining both training attempts in audit data.
5. Public awareness now distinguishes ability possession from confirmed exposure. Awakening, advanced Haki, Mythical Zoan identity, and sensitive traits require explicit exposure, a public battle plus a relevant public reputation, or a relevant Government record.
6. The capture-cost anchors now concentrate value in the actual high-danger range: 65/70/75/80/85 map to 240M/350M/500M/700M/950M before awareness confidence is applied. Confidence multipliers are unknown 0.20, rumored 0.65, emerging 0.85, known 1.00, infamous 1.10, and world-famous 1.20.

## World Government assessment model

The bounty system treats a high combat rating as an internal simulator measurement, not automatic Government knowledge. The Marine-facing assessment uses recorded accomplishments, Government/Marine incidents, role, reputation, influence, and explicitly exposed abilities. Therefore, a very strong but secretive character can still have a comparatively low bounty; this is intentional rather than a tier clamp failure.

The revised capture floor is meaningful when the Government has enough evidence: 4 of the 9 characters in the 70–79 Combat Danger band activated it. It remains rare across the full sample because only 26 of 100,000 characters reached danger 60+, and hidden or unknown combatants are deliberately discounted.

## Decision

V4.4.1 corrects the identified implementation defects. The bounty tail should still be monitored in future content batches, but no further formula change is justified by this sample: high-power secretive characters remain low-bounty by design, while publicly evidenced high-danger characters now receive a material capture-cost floor.
