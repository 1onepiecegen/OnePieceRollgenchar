# V4.5.2 Sandbox World Intelligence

## Scope

This pass keeps Standard combat, progression, awakening, mentor rarity, and the V4.4.1 bounty anchors intact. It adds a separate Marine-information layer: actual capability, Government knowledge, and displayed bounty are now distinct concepts.

## Sandbox world construction

Sandbox supports World Presence presets from Hidden Monster through Pirate King and World Government Nightmare. A presence preset can supply Captain status, crew quality, appropriate accomplishment record, reputation, influence, political danger, criminal record, and strategic attention. Explicit role and reputation selections still take priority.

Government Knowledge supports derived intelligence, no intelligence, rumors, partial/major leaks, full dossier, false report, and custom intelligence. Custom intelligence accepts exposed fact IDs and coverage/reliability values. Full dossiers expose all applicable facts; they do not create powers the character does not possess.

## Bounty representation

The bounty report now preserves:

- `calculatedBounty`: the Marine assessment calculated from the character and known information;
- `creatorBountyOverride`: an optional Unrestricted Sandbox author value;
- `displayedBounty`: the value shown on the wanted poster.

An override never replaces the calculated assessment in Behind the Scenes.

## Intelligence reports

`governmentIntelligence` stores leak status, source, coverage, reliability, exposed facts, hidden facts, potentially false facts, reasons, and the derived awareness/threat effects. Standard characters roll a rare post-generation intelligence event; the chance rises with visible behavior and Government conflict. Combat Rating itself never changes because of a report.
