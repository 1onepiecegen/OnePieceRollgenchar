## Canon-sensitive battle rules

- Fire is not assigned a general weakness or low rank. The Mera Mera no Mi remains a powerful Logia.
- Magma receives a specific offensive affinity over ordinary flame. This models the demonstrated magma/flame interaction without treating fire as broadly weak.
- Darkness forces Devil Fruit defenses into contact and can nullify fruit powers while contact is maintained.
- Marshall D. Teach's profile carries the Yami Yami no Mi and Gura Gura no Mi. The second-fruit allowance is a character-specific canon exception whose mechanism is explicitly recorded as unknown; the Yami Yami no Mi does not grant two fruits to ordinary generated characters.
- Every other generated or career character is rejected when attempting to acquire a second ordinary Devil Fruit.
- Affinity rules are simulator abstractions for the Experimental battle engine, not a claim that canon uses a universal elemental type chart.

## User interface

- Battle selection now uses a filterable roster of Saved Crew, canon profiles, and OC/fan profiles instead of the old dropdowns.
- The Experimental resolver uses deterministic seeds, stamina, initiative, behavior tendencies, Haki contact, Logia interaction, awakenings, weapons, multiple-fruit profiles, and visible affinity notes.
- Sandbox includes savable character-copy presets for every reviewed profile.
- Character sheets expose fruit rarity and affinities, true identity when relevant, signature abilities, and editable character names. Changing a name does not change the character ID, seed, or Build ID.
- Goose begins with a displayed bounty of zero because the world has not verified her existence; the calculated danger can still be retained behind the scenes.
