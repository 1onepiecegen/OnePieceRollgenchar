# V5.3 Battle Sessions and Fruit Batch

Database version: `5.3.0-battle-session-and-fruit-batch`  
Spoiler boundary: end of Wano

## Delivered

- Fixed Sandbox character-copy presets with incomplete provenance metadata. The Katakuri preset now supplies a safe provenance record, and older imported saves render safely when provenance fields are absent.
- Added 24 explicit, stable-ID fan/original Devil Fruit records in `database/fruits-batch-v5.3.js`. Their source is visibly `fan`; uncommon, rare, legendary, mythic, and anomaly concepts have correspondingly low standard-generation weights.
- Added a distinct Battle Match page. Players can choose Player 1 vs AI, Player 2 vs AI, or two AI fighters, then use visible actions for style, Haki, weapon, fruit, and recovery.
- Reworked the experimental resolver around deterministic battle sessions. It records turns, HP, stamina, actions, interaction notes, and a final winner or draw.
- Added animated arena and impact effects without affecting standard generation or any V5.0 baseline formula.
