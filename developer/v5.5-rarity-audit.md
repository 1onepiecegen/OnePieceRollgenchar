# V5.5 Rarity Audit

Natural generation remains eligible for rarity reporting. Standard and Brutal results use a conditional estimate label with an explicit statement that linked events are correlated.

- Authored canon presets: natural rarity not applicable.
- Authored OC presets: natural rarity not applicable.
- Unrestricted Sandbox: natural rarity not applicable.
- Guided Sandbox: conditional build estimate only.
- Player-facing values use percentages and “About 1 in N”; anomaly-level values use “Less than 1 in 1 billion.”

The 100,000 generation sample completed with 70,157 fruit users, no invalid second-fruit records, and no generation failures. V5.5 does not retune the existing V5.4 rarity or roll-mode tables.
