## Marine intelligence model

Awareness is derived from visible accomplishments, government/Military incidents, reputation, crew position and influence, known rare abilities, and secrecy-oriented role/reputation signals. It deliberately does **not** roll independently and does not alter the underlying Marine-threat calculation.

The available copied reference describes the World Government’s global affiliation and its supervision of the Marines, as well as the importance of major government facilities and public incidents. The implementation uses those broad facts only; it does not introduce post-Wano story content or secret-government knowledge as a player-facing system.
