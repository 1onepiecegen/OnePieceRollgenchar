## Scope and freeze

V5.1 adds the data contracts required for future encounters, battle, and career play. It does not retune Combat Rating, Marine Threat, bounty, rarity, standard-generation weights, awakening, Sandbox construction, or loadout logic.

Career/reference content is external to the standard roll pools. In particular, the reviewed Moku Moku no Mi record supports Smoker and explicit career acquisition, but is held in `careerFruits` rather than appended to the frozen standard fruit array.

## Reviewed first opponent batch

The initial curated simulator-profile batch contains Monkey D. Luffy, Roronoa Zoro, Sanji, Trafalgar Law, Smoker, Rob Lucci, Charlotte Katakuri, and Dracule Mihawk. A profile keeps physical, mental, Haki, fruit, weapon, discipline, advanced-state, and behavior information separate. Its numbers are clearly labelled simulator interpretations, not official power rankings.

Character references and mentors remain separate. A mentor now links to a valid character reference through `characterId`; being a reference character does not itself make someone an eligible mentor. The relationship data includes only direct, recorded relationships in this first batch; no hypothetical mentor chains were added.

## Career foundation

Every newly generated character receives a `careerProgression` record without rerolling any identity field. Imported V4/V5 saves receive the same empty/default structure on load.

The optional Career interface supports deterministic, bounded actions for Haki, weapon and discipline practice, recovery, mentor search, fruit exploration, rival challenge, Marine enlistment, and Cipher Pol candidacy. It preserves seed, identity, equipment continuity, and history. Training has capped experience and does not silently grant advanced Haki, awakening, techniques, or advanced states.

`acquireDevilFruit(character, fruitId, context)` is available for a later explicit world event. It refuses a second ordinary fruit, respects Crew-uniqueness context, starts mastery low, records the event, and never grants awakening by acquisition.

Techniques are now standalone data. The progression event module evaluates parent disciplines and requirement fields from each technique record, and explicit learning validates the permitted route (`training`, `mentor`, or `battle-insight`).

## Battle preparation

`combatant-adapter.js` normalizes both saved/generated characters and curated profiles into a shared battle-ready shape. Reviewed canon profiles are selectable in the existing Battle Arena, marked **Experimental** because the old visual resolver is retained only as a compatibility preview. No turn-based Battle Engine was implemented.

`encounter-resolver.js` filters the small location encounter tables by sea, Combat Rating band, Marine Heat, source, and spoiler boundary. It is a selection foundation only; it does not yet run a full combat encounter, recruitment, or territory-ownership loop.

## Known limitations and next step

- The first combat-profile batch is intentionally small and reviewed; more opponents need the same evidence and validation process.
- Career fruit exploration deliberately reports only a search result until a specific world-event acquisition route is reviewed.
- Marine and Cipher Pol paths record career intent and entry rank, but missions, promotions, and covert assignments belong to the next Career/Battle pass.
- Battle Arena canon selection is an experimental compatibility bridge, not an authoritative combat simulation.
- No new fan fruit has been introduced in this pass. Existing fan records remain classified and untouched in the frozen standard pool.

The recommended next step is a dedicated Battle Engine design review: turn structure, behavior profiles, costs, tactics, encounter stakes, loss/injury consequences, and a clear separation between simulator outcomes and canon claims.
