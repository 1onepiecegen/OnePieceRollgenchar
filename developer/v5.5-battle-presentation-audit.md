# V5.5 Battle Presentation Audit

The deterministic battle engine remains separate from presentation.

- Phases: decision, telegraph, counter window, impact, resolution, aftermath.
- Pacing: Fast, Normal, Cinematic, and Manual phase advance.
- Cinematic target holds: decision 650ms, telegraph 1200ms, counter 900ms, impact 600ms, resolution 1100ms, aftermath 900ms.
- Existing visual impact classes continue to cover impact, Haki, and Fruit overlays.
- Accessibility modes: full effects, reduce motion/shake, and no flash.

No timing value participates in RNG, action selection, damage, or replay data.
