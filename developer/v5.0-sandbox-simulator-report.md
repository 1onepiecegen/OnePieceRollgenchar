# V5.0 One Piece Sandbox Simulator

V4.5.3 is preserved as the last V4 integrity baseline. V5 begins a new, coordinated scale and world-simulation layer.

## Phase 1 — Infinite Scaling

- Combat Rating has no displayed ceiling. Ordinary rolls still use the existing nonlinear normalization range; scores above it continue along an open linear tail.
- Marine Threat is now an open operational-priority score rather than a 0–100 percentage. Combat, politics, influence, criminal record, strategic value, and volatility remain distinct inputs.
- Official bounty extrapolates smoothly past the last calibration anchor. No maximum bounty is applied.
- V5 tiers are: East Blue Rookie, Grand Line Rookie, Super Rookie, Supernova, Rising Threat, Commander, Yonko Commander, Admiral-Class, Fleet Admiral, Emperor, and open-ended Pirate King.
- Unrestricted Creator includes an exact Combat Rating target. It constructs the requested one-point target band directly and does not search seeds.

## Phase 2 — Rarity and Records

- Every generated character receives a transparent rarity estimate with a component breakdown and combined approximate odds.
- The estimate is deliberately labelled as an approximation: combinations are not treated as independent statistical guarantees.
- Local-only personal records track highest Combat Rating, Marine Threat, bounty, strongest sword user, strongest Devil Fruit user, and rarest observed roll.
- Unrestricted alternate-universe builds do not overwrite normal-roll personal records.

## Phase 3 — World Immersion

- The character sheet contains a Marine HQ Intelligence Report: classification, threat/capture priority, confirmed and hidden facts, reports/rumors, observed incidents, last-seen area, and a proportionate response recommendation.
- This renders the existing Government intelligence data. It does not reveal facts the dossier has not confirmed.

## Phase 4 — First Living Sandbox

- Each displayed character receives a persistent voyage state keyed to its character identity.
- The first playable loop generates a crew-role roster, territory control and standing, an active rival profile, a Marine pursuit posture, heat, morale, and an event history.
- **Advance Voyage** resolves a deterministic next event from the character seed and current voyage turn. This is intentionally a first living-world layer, leaving combat encounters, crew recruitment, and territory ownership for later V5 content passes.

## Knowledge boundary

The V5 presentation uses ordinary One Piece terms such as Marines, Haki, Devil Fruits, and Grand Line/New World geography. It adds no speculative canon power rankings and keeps its existing late-Wano spoiler boundary. The requested Sportskeeda One Piece wiki was consulted for terminology and presentation context, including its entries on [Haki and Devil Fruit combat context](https://wiki.sportskeeda.com/one-piece/magu-magu-no-mi-in-one-piece) and [advanced Observation Haki](https://wiki.sportskeeda.com/one-piece/charlotte-katakuri-one-piece).
